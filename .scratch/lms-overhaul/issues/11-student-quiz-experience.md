# 11: Student quiz experience

**What to build:** A quiz page with one-column, full-width answers, a submit confirmation, and a result view showing the grade and correct answers. Reopening an already-submitted quiz shows the answers instead of allowing a re-solve.

**Blocked by:** 01, 02, 04.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] MCQ answers render full width in a single column; true/false is equally usable.
- [ ] Submit asks for confirmation, then shows the score and correct answers.
- [ ] A second visit shows the previous result and answers, not a fresh form.
- [ ] Enrollment and lesson-unlock rules are enforced on the server, including direct URL access.
